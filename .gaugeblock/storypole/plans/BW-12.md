## 1. Executive Summary & Root Cause Analysis

### Identified Drift & Code Duplication
1. **Chat Duplication & Drift:**
   * Panel 1 embedded `EngineerChat.tsx`, which bypassed the chat harness and context injection engine entirely, making a direct call to `api.pingModel('Planner', textToSend)`.
   * Meanwhile, `App.tsx` mounted a persistent `CopilotRail.tsx` on the right side calling `/api/v1/chat/stream`.
   * In the backend (`benchwork-cli/src/daemon/routes_chat.rs`), `/api/v1/chat/stream` was never wired to real Sawhorse inference—it returned a hardcoded mock delta string: `"Context initialized for ... on panel ..."`.
2. **Naming & Trademark Conflicts:**
   * "Copilot" is a Microsoft trademark.
   * "/grill-me" is an Antigravity CLI skill rather than a standalone product name.
3. **Broken Context Injection:**
   * Selecting `@task:KEY` or `@doc:ARCHETYPE` pills was supposed to inject live markdown plans, document previews, and AST signatures into the prompt before passing to Sawhorse, but because the backend endpoint was mocked, injection never took effect.
